03 / BACKGROUND

The Lore

It is said that the String Alphabet was once used by a forgotten civilization, passed down through generations in whispers, notes, and private messages. Some believe it was created by people who could not freely express their feelings, so they developed a language understood only by the close, the trusted, or the intended.

Over time, the alphabet became more than a way to write. It became a symbol of love, loyalty, betrayal, and even revenge. Today, it is rarely seen. Some say it is still used in secret, written at the edge of letters, in the margins of books, or in messages meant for one person alone. No one knows for sure where it came from, or when it will disappear again.
